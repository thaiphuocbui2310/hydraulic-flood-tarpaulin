# Smart Hydraulic Flood Tarpaulin

> 🏆 **Finalist** — EPICS Community Solution Competition  
> 📅 Jan 2024 &nbsp;|&nbsp; Team of 5

---

## 📌 Overview

A **smart hydraulic flood protection cover** designed to protect high-value household appliances (TVs, refrigerators, washing machines) in flood-prone areas of Vietnam — without requiring residents to manually move heavy equipment.

The cover uses **hydraulic water barriers** that inflate automatically when flooding is detected, creating a watertight seal around appliances. After the flood, water drains out and the cover folds compactly for storage.

---

## 💡 Problem Statement

Flooding is a recurring disaster in central and southern Vietnam. Each flood season, families lose thousands of dollars worth of household appliances that cannot be moved quickly enough. Existing solutions (sandbags, waterproof bags) are either too slow, too bulky, or not effective enough.

---

## 🏗️ Design Concept

```
Normal State (Stored):
┌─────────────────────┐
│  Folded Tarpaulin   │  ← Compact, stored flat
│  (deflated barriers)│
└─────────────────────┘

Flood State (Deployed):
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│  💧 Water Barrier  │ Appliance │  ← 4 hydraulic barriers
│  (inflated)        │  (dry ✓)  │     form a watertight box
│  💧 Water Barrier  │           │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Key principles:**
- 4 hydraulic water barriers around the perimeter
- Hydrostatic pressure keeps barriers rigid under flood water
- Drain valves allow quick deflation after flooding
- Folds to 1/8 of deployed size for storage

## 🛠️ Design Components

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Tarpaulin shell | Heavy-duty waterproof fabric |
| Water barriers | 4× inflatable hydraulic chambers |
| Drain system | Manual drain valves |
| Frame | Lightweight aluminum skeleton |

## 🔬 Engineering Principles Applied

- **Hydrostatics**: Water pressure analysis to size barrier walls
- **Structural design**: Load distribution under flood conditions
- **Fluid mechanics**: Drain rate and valve sizing calculations
- **Product design**: Ergonomics, packaging, ease of use
